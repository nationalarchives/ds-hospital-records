What is this service?

This service provides information about the location and coverage of hospital records across the UK. It was developed by The National Archives in partnership with the Wellcome Trust. For more information about the project, see About this project.

How to use this service

  1. Search for a hospital by name, previous names, or town.
  2. Open a hospital to see where records are held.
  3. Follow repository links for contact details and access notes.

What you will find

  • The administrative details of the hospitals, and their status or type.
  • The location and covering dates of administrative and clinical records.
  • The existence of lists, catalogues or other finding aids.
  • Links to some online catalogues on Discovery.

Need help?

If you cannot find a hospital, try searching a previous hospital name or nearby town. Some hospitals may be listed under a different name.

For access queries, contact the repository directly using the details on its page.
