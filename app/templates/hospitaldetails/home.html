Hospital records

Explore hospital history across the UK. Search by hospital name or town, or browse repositories that hold surviving records.

Search hospitals Browse repositories

How to use this service

  1. Search for a hospital by name, previous names, or town.
  2. Open a hospital to see where records are held.
  3. Follow repository links for contact details and access notes.

What you will find

  • Admissions, registers, and administrative records (where known).
  • Repository codes and Archon references to support enquiries.
  • Notes on access or research requirements when available.

Need help?

If you cannot find a record, try searching a previous hospital name or nearby town. Some records are unlisted or held privately.

For access queries, contact the repository directly using the details on its page.
